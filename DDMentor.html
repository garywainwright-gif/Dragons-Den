What is the Dragon's Den game?

Dragon's Den is a TV show where people with a business idea — called — try to convince wealthy investors to invest money in the entrepreneur's business. An entrepreneur has a few minutes to explain what they sell, who it is for, and how much money they need. This explanation is called the .

Investors do not give gifts. If they like the idea, they may invest — put money into the business — but they want a share of the business in return. This is called . Your job in the game as an entrepreneur is to invent the product and write the pitch. You will meet the investors — the dragons — when your product pitch is ready.

How you get ready

Play your own game Create a unique product from your imagination or use the game’s idea generator. Work through the steps to build the features and benefits of your product, to decide who your customers are and how you will reach them, and get the costing and prices right. Then test your pitch in the Dragons’ Den when you answer questions to get investment.

Develop your target customers

Name a real group of people who you think are likely to buy your product, they are your future customers. Try to find out what problem your product solves for these customers, where will this specific group of people be able to find your product easily, which retail markets do they visit and how they will hear about the product.

Investors always ask: who will actually buy this? “Everyone” is too vague. You can write in the boxes, or tap Speak my answers once and say each part out loud (Chrome or Edge).

Your pitch — six stages

A pitch is a short presentation which is used to explain who you are and what your product is. The following steps will help you to build the pitch you will make to the Dragons. The finished pitch will also include the information from the pages on money, customers and marketing. The mentor will then read your pitch back to you to enable you to make amendments before presenting to the Dragons.

A pitch is a short speech in a set order. Write each stage in your own words from the product you invented — or tap Speak my answers once and say each stage out loud (Chrome or Edge). There is no sample product on this page.

Round 2 — The marketplace

The Dragons now ask how the business actually runs: getting it made, moved, and supplied. Expect questions on logistics, suppliers, and the cost of goods. A thin first answer still means you study the advice and answer once more. After that second try they move on, even if the new answer is still thin.
